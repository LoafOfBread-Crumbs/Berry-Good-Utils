# Berry Good Utils Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Berry Good Utils is a private business utility application. It does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, telemetry, or tracking.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores customer, scheduling, catalogue, inventory, supplier, and company information entered by the user. Application settings and records are stored locally in `%APPDATA%\BerryGoodUtils`. Customer and business documents are stored locally under `Documents\BerryGoodUtils`.

The app does not automatically send this locally stored business information to the developer.

## Google services

Google integration is optional. If the user chooses to sign in, Berry Good Utils requests access needed to:

- send messages through Gmail;
- read Gmail message metadata used by app features;
- create and manage calendar events created by the app; and
- create or read Google Drive files selected or managed through app features.

Google account data is exchanged directly between the app and Google APIs for user-requested features. OAuth tokens are encrypted for the current Windows user and stored locally in `%APPDATA%\BerryGoodUtils\GoogleTokens`. The app never asks for or stores the user's Google password.

Berry Good Utils' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Photos and files

Photos and files selected or downloaded by the user may be copied into the applicable local customer folder. The app does not upload those files to the developer.

## Data sharing

The developer does not sell user information. Information is shared with Google only when necessary to perform a Google-integrated action requested by the user. Information may also be included in emails or documents that the user explicitly chooses to create, export, or send.

## Data retention and deletion

Data remains on the user's computer until the user deletes it. To remove local app data, delete `%APPDATA%\BerryGoodUtils`. To remove customer and business documents, delete the applicable content under `Documents\BerryGoodUtils`. Google authorization can be revoked with the app's **Sign out** button or from the user's Google Account permissions page.

Uninstalling the app may not remove separately stored business documents or application data, allowing them to survive updates or reinstallation.

## Security

Google OAuth tokens are protected with Windows Data Protection for the current Windows user. Users are responsible for securing their Windows account, device, exported documents, and recipient information.

## Children

Berry Good Utils is a business utility and is not directed to children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
